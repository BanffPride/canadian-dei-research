Banff Pride Partnerships: Operating Guide (v1.0, 6 September 2026)

WHAT THIS IS
One workbook that tracks every sponsor and partner from prospect to renewal. It replaces the July prospect sheet, the January pipeline and in-kind workbooks, the August donation tracker and the census tracker, which are now archived. Drive holds documents. Gmail, Granola, Todoist and Calendar hold their own records and are linked by ID.

FIRST TIME (one minute)
1. Open "Banff Pride Partnerships.xlsx" in 04 Sponsorship Delivery.
2. File > Save as Google Sheets. Rename the new Sheet "Banff Pride Partnerships". Trash the .xlsx.
3. Check the ED Dashboard: the two self-check numbers at the bottom should read 154 organizations and 100 deliverables. If any view shows #NAME?, tell Claude.

DAILY (two minutes, phone or desktop)
1. ED Dashboard. Nine exception counts, each naming the tab to open. Zero means nothing needs you.
2. "Recommended next actions" lists the ten most urgent deliverables Banff Pride owes, earliest first.
3. "Top open relationships" lists the ten nearest next-action dates.

WEEKLY (fifteen minutes)
1. Decisions Required: set Status to Approved, Rejected or Deferred in the Decisions tab.
2. Deliverables Due: anything due in 14 days. Mark Delivered and verified only after adding an Evidence row and its ID.
3. Follow-up Needed: communications Banff Pride owes a reply on within 7 days.
4. Data Quality: counts that should trend to zero.
5. Contributions: enter Received date and amount from Wave for any cash that landed.

RULES THE WORKBOOK ENFORCES
- Four status fields, not one: Opportunity stage, Agreement status, Payment or in-kind status (computed), Fulfilment status per deliverable.
- Confirmed requires written confirmation; Closed won requires a signed agreement link. Agreements without a document are flagged.
- A deliverable reported done without an evidence link stays flagged as Missing evidence.
- In-kind rows need a valuation basis. Promo codes, discounts and free inclusions are in-kind (ED rule, 5 September). Unvalued rows are counted, not summed.
- Cash figures are entered from Wave. The workbook is not a ledger.
- Contacts is restricted. Do not share or export it.
- Nothing is emailed from the workbook. ThankYouEmail only records recipients and approvals.

RECORD TABS (green): Organizations, Contacts, Opportunities, Agreements, Contributions, Deliverables, Evidence, Communications, BrandAssets, Tickets, Events, Metrics, Reports, Renewals, Decisions, Improvements, ThankYouEmail.
SYSTEM TABS (grey): AuditLog, SourceIndex, Lists (dropdown values and config: warning days, stale days, festival dates).
VIEW TABS (blue, formulas only): ED Dashboard, Decisions Required, Directory, Prospect Pipeline, Confirmed Support, Deliverables Due, Deliverables at Risk, Blocked, Missing Evidence, Follow-up Needed, Brand Assets View, Events View, Reporting Readiness, Renewal Pipeline, Data Quality.

ADDING A NEW PARTNER
1. Organizations: new row, next ORG number, public name, relationship types.
2. Opportunities: new row with stage, priority, next action, owner and date.
3. When an agreement is signed: Agreements row with the PDF link, then one Contributions row per cash or in-kind line, then one Deliverables row per benefit (copy the tier's standard benefits from the agreement template).
4. Logos go to Partnerships / Brand Asset Library / [Partner ID] Partner Name / 01 Current Approved Logos. Record the link in BrandAssets.

BRAND ASSET LIBRARY (Partnerships at the top level of the BP shared drive)
[Partner ID] Partner Name / 01 Current Approved Logos (Primary, Reversed or White, Monochrome, Vector) / 02 Brand Guidelines / 03 Approval Records / 99 Expired - Do Not Use.
Use only files in 01. Never delete an expired logo; move it to 99. Use Drive shortcuts in event and campaign folders. When Banff Pride's logo appears with partners, the Brand Book rules apply: Banff Pride leads, stacked logo, divider lines, clear space. BLLT approval lead times: 10 business days major assets, 3 routine, 5 web.

No approval, no send.

PARTNER REPORTS
Reports tab tracks one report per confirmed partner (BLLT Final Report due 12 November in Appendix B format; others by 30 November). A report only claims what Deliverables shows as Delivered and verified with evidence. Organization-wide impact is labelled separately from partner-attributable metrics (Metrics tab, Scope column).

WHAT CLAUDE DOES AUTOMATICALLY (read-only unless approved)
Reads the Gmail Sponsorship label, Granola and Todoist since the last checkpoint; proposes new commitments into Communications with a Proposed records column; runs the exception report; never sends, never edits signed agreements, never marks a contractual deliverable complete without evidence.

WHAT NEEDS YOUR APPROVAL
Creating Todoist tasks, scheduling communications, changing financial values, moving Drive files, changing schema or status lists, any automation that writes.
